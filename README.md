### [Plated](https://joinplated.app/)

A cross-platform (iOS, Android, web) social food-discovery app built in Expo/React Native and TypeScript. Rates individual **dishes** instead of restaurants, aggregates dish-level ratings into a restaurant's overall score, and uses **Reorder** counts as a trust signal. Backed by Supabase (auth, Postgres, storage), the Foursquare Places API, and **Platos** — a short-form video feed with its own upload pipeline. Building toward an FTC-compliant creator-commission model and App Store readiness, with Stripe Connect payouts planned.
